# VJ Intelligence: technical document (More than a Hack 2027, Initial Challenge)

Team VJ Intelligence. Repository: https://github.com/VJ-Intelligence/mtah2027-submission. Submission file: `submissions/VJ Intelligence.csv`,
regenerated by `make submission`.

## 1. Approach in one paragraph

We never learn anything from the provided match. Every model is trained on publicly released
external matches (99 in total, four providers) whose possession labels we convert to the challenge's "controlled possession"
semantics, after a *degrader* has made their clean tracking look like the challenge's automatic
tracking (missing and smoothed ball, untracked off-pitch ball, false detections, noisy and
dropping players, no identities). Four dilated temporal convolution networks (TCNs), all trained on
DFL, SoccerTrack v2 and the PFF FC World Cup 2022 tracking, three also on SkillCorner, with
different seeds and mixes, read 81 identity-free, team-symmetric features per frame; their
averaged probabilities P(D, H, A) are decoded into possession spells by a Viterbi decoder with
switch penalties. One generic rule from the Smart Tagging sets the ball dead before each half's
kickoff. Everything was selected on a leave-one-match-out bench over external matches scored with
the official scorer.

Why this and not something else: spellF1 rewards whole possessions, so the decisive part is a
temporal model plus a decoder that does not fragment spells. With no labels on the target match,
the only lever on generalisation is making the training data look like the target data
(domain randomisation) and keeping features free of anything that differs between tracking
systems (identities, slot order, absolute speeds of jittery tracks).

## 2. Training data (all external, none from the provided game)

| Dataset | Matches | Licence | Labels: who / how | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DFL / IDSSE open data (Bundesliga) | 7 | CC BY 4.0 | DFL official data provider: ball status + ball possession feed, plus pass/shot events | main training set and bench |
| SkillCorner open data (A-League 2024/25) | 20 | MIT (credit SkillCorner) | SkillCorner computer vision: phases of play + possession group | training (one TCN), domain-gap check |
| SoccerTrack v2 (university matches) | 8 of 10 | CC BY 4.0 (cite the SoccerTrack v2 paper) | provider annotations: ball-action events with acting team + ball status | training (TCNs) |
| PFF FC FIFA Men's World Cup 2022 | 64 | PFF FC public release for research, education and analytics (free registration) | PFF event analysts: on-the-ball events with team + out-of-play events; broadcast tracking (Sportlogiq) | training (all TCNs) |
| Metrica Sports sample game 3 | 1 | Metrica sample data licence | none used | EPTS format testing only |

No labels were produced by us; no data was hand-labelled. Label conversion (code in
`src/mtah/labels.py`, `src/mtah/external/*.py`):

- DFL: ball status alive/dead gives D; the possession feed gives H/A. The feed flips on touches,
  so an opponent spell shorter than 1 s, sandwiched in one team's possession, without a pass or
  shot by the opponent inside it, is merged back (controlled possession).
- SkillCorner: outside phases of play = D; inside = possession group; short ungrouped gaps
  between same-team spells are loose balls and keep the team.
- SoccerTrack v2: possession = team of the latest possession event (pass, drive, cross, shot,
  restart, successful tackle, header) while the provider's ball status says the ball is alive;
  same controlled-possession merge. Two matches without a ball-status feed are excluded.
- PFF WC2022 (`src/mtah/external/pff.py`): D from an OUT event (ball out or whistle) until the
  next restart is taken; otherwise the team of the latest on-the-ball event, so the previous team
  keeps the ball while it travels; same controlled-possession merge with passes, crosses, shots
  and carries as deliberate actions. Raw tracking positions are used, not PFF's smoothed ones
  (their smoothed ball is placed on the event's ball carrier, which would leak event timing into
  the input). 29.97 fps is resampled to 25 fps on the video clock. Event times trail the tracking
  by ~0.3 to 0.55 s at possession changes; shifting the labels was measured and not adopted.
  We train only on our degraded copies; the PFF files themselves are not in this repository.

## 4. Features and degrader

Features (`src/mtah/features.py`), per frame, identity-free and mirrored for team-swap
augmentation and symmetric inference: distance of each team's nearest players to the ball,
pressure counts within 3 and 6 m, team centroids, spread and forward velocity, ball speed,
acceleration and position, ball-missing run lengths, touch candidates (ball acceleration peaks)
with the nearer team, all at several time scales (0.5 to 10 s, past, future and centred
windows). Team velocity is the median displacement of individual players (nearest-neighbour match
5 frames back), which is robust to players dropping in and out of the tracking; the centroid
derivative is not.

Degrader (`src/mtah/degrade.py`), randomised per match copy (domain randomisation): aerial ball
projected along the camera ray with random strength; ball noise and centred or causal smoothing
(13 to 25 frames); ball gaps concentrated in stoppages plus in-play gaps triggered by aerial and
crowded situations; off-pitch ball mostly untracked; rare false ball detections; temporally
correlated player position error; player dropouts and duplicate tracks; slot shuffling.
Parameter ranges are centred on the descriptive statistics published in the official `DATA.md`
(ball present on ~59% of frames, missing frames concentrated in stoppages, ~17-frame smoothing,
~20.5 of 22 players tracked). For PFF broadcast tracking, players the
provider estimated off-screen are kept (the challenge tracking covers almost all 22), the
estimated ball is blanked and counted towards the ball-missing target, so the degrader only adds
the remaining gaps. We additionally checked, without any labels, that per-feature
means of the provided tracking fall inside the range spanned by our degraded external matches
(`python -m mtah.checks shift`) and widened ranges where they did not (off-pitch ball tracking,
ball noise, false detections).

## 5. Models and decoding

- Four non-causal dilated TCNs (hidden 96, 12 residual blocks, receptive field ~±20 s, 6 epochs),
  trained on 1,500-frame windows of 4 degraded copies per DFL match, team-swap augmented, plus the
  8 SoccerTrack v2 matches and the 64 PFF WC2022 matches (one degraded copy each): three also add
  the 20 SkillCorner matches (two seeds with one SoccerTrack copy, one with three copies), one is
  DFL + SoccerTrack + PFF only. Equal weights. Auxiliary head: possession-change probability (multi-task; not
  used in decoding). Symmetric inference (model and team-swapped mirror averaged).
- A LightGBM frame classifier was part of earlier versions; once the TCNs were in it added
  nothing (any weight lowered the bench score), so it was dropped.
- Decoding: Viterbi per half, penalty 8 for an H/A switch and 24 for entering or leaving D.
  Minimum-duration constraints and separate enter/exit penalties were tested and gave no gain.
  This is the decoding step of a hybrid neural-network/HMM: states D/H/A, the TCN's per-frame
  probabilities as emissions, the switch penalties as (log) transition costs. A classic HMM with
  hand-made emissions (e.g. nearest-player distance) is the same decoder with a much weaker
  emission model.

## 6. Results on the external bench (leave one DFL match out, official scorer)

| Variant | spellF1 |
|---|---|
| Official baseline approach (nearest player, best window) | 0.24 |
| LightGBM + Viterbi | 0.54 |
| TCN, DFL only | 0.629 |
| TCN, DFL + SkillCorner | 0.637 |
| TCN, DFL + SkillCorner + SoccerTrack | 0.661 / 0.665 (two seeds) |
| Previous ensemble (4 TCNs with SoccerTrack, no PFF) | 0.676 |
| TCN, DFL + SkillCorner + SoccerTrack + PFF WC2022 | 0.691 |
| **Final ensemble (4 TCNs, all with PFF)** | **0.696** |

Adding SoccerTrack improved every one of the 7 held-out matches (+0.010 to +0.033). Adding the
64 PFF matches raised the single TCN from 0.661 to 0.691 (6 of 7 matches better, one -0.005).
The equal-weight ensemble adds +0.005 over its best member (4 of 7 matches). A weight grid
found 0.701 for one mix, but it was selected on the same 7 matches, so we kept equal weights.
Per held-out match (final ensemble): 0.691, 0.674, 0.729, 0.689, 0.664, 0.687, 0.738.

Tried and dropped: touch features as a separate signal (no gain), boundary-weighted loss (no
gain), change-head-driven decoding (+0.001), SkillCorner in LightGBM (worse), more epochs
(9 instead of 6: 0.641, overfits), three instead of one degraded SoccerTrack copy (within seed
noise), adding a TCN without SoccerTrack to the final ensemble (worse on 7/7 matches), LightGBM in
the ensemble (worse), minimum-duration and asymmetric-penalty decoding (+0.001, noise), a wider
TCN (hidden 128: 0.650), 6 instead of 4 degraded DFL copies (0.656), extra loss weight on short
spells (0.657), input dropout with stronger weight decay (0.653), weight averaging over the last
two epochs (+0.003 on those), a 6-member ensemble (+0.002, 4/7 matches), inserting short
confident opponent spells after decoding (worse in every setting) and shifting labels in time
(no lag: shift 0 is best). Accept rule throughout: mean gain above ~0.005 and most held-out
matches better.

Domain gap: DFL-trained models on 20 held-out SkillCorner matches scored 0.35 vs 0.20 for the
baseline approach; mixing SkillCorner into training raised held-out SkillCorner to 0.45 without
hurting DFL. We expect the provided match to land between the two worlds.

## 7. How the public 5 minutes were used

Only to check that the pipeline runs end to end, as intended by the organisers. It caught two
bugs that the external bench could not see, both fixed generically: (1) feature rows were
reordered when a match's periods are not contiguous (the provided match has ungraded frames
before, between and after the halves); (2) the degrader's player dropouts inflated team speed
2.5x compared with real tracking. No parameter, threshold or model choice was selected on it.

## 8. Possession rules we implemented

- Possession changes only when the other team gains control: short opponent touches without a
  pass or shot (under 1 s) are not a change.
- Loose balls and duels: the previous team keeps possession until the other team controls the
  ball (labels and the Viterbi switch penalty both encode this).
- Restarts and stoppages: D while the ball is out of play, from the external providers' ball
  status (ball out or whistle) until the restart is taken; the restarting team then has the ball.
- Before each half's kickoff: D.

## 9. Limitations

- Short possessions remain hard: on the bench, spells under 1 s are matched 15% of the time,
  1 to 2 s 27%, 2 to 4 s 49%.
- Label semantics differ between providers (DFL feed vs Metrica's controlled possession); our
  conversion rules are approximations.
- The domain gap: optical tracking from several providers, but none is the challenge's own
  tracking system; PFF is broadcast tracking with many estimated off-screen players.
- PFF event times trail its tracking slightly; we left the labels unshifted (measured better).
- Training on the Apple GPU (PyTorch MPS) is not bit-exact; see section 10.

With more time: more diverse external leagues, a touch-level event detector trained on event
data, a segment-level decoder, and self-training on unlabelled external tracking.

## 10. Reproduction

```bash
git clone --recursive https://github.com/VJ-Intelligence/mtah2027-submission && cd mtah2027-submission
make setup                                    # Python 3.11, pinned dependencies
export MTAH_DATA_DIR=/path/to/unpacked/bundle  # contains tactical/
make submission && make validate              # -> submissions/VJ Intelligence.csv
```

The trained models are committed in `models/`. `make submission` runs on CPU with deterministic
algorithms and reproduces the file byte for byte (MD5 b7f877b166c9b24a673c008b8dbf4670 for the current models, verified over two runs). `make train` rebuilds the models
from the external data (downloaded by the loaders in `src/mtah/external/`; the PFF WC2022
release needs a free registration with PFF FC, then `python -m mtah.external.pff` converts it
under `MTAH_PFF_DIR`); the TCNs are trained on the Apple GPU, which is not bit-exact, so a retrain gives
slightly different weights. `DEVICE=cpu make train` is deterministic but slower.
